# Related work for few-shot adaptation versus retrieval

Updated 25 September 2026. The active question is in [RESEARCH.md](../RESEARCH.md); implementation state is in [STATUS.md](../STATUS.md).

## Candidate question

Given equal support demonstrations of a new LIBERO task, does feature adaptation through LoRA improve visual inverse-dynamics action prediction beyond retrieval or action-head-only fine-tuning, and what compute cost does each incur? This is a proposed comparison, not a verified literature gap. The contribution would have to come from a clear condition-dependent finding, fair protocol, or measured performance/cost tradeoff.

## Claim–evidence comparison

| [TAIL (ICLR 2024)](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/46e2b525da536030ec1589dddea01feb-Paper-Conference.pdf) | Compares LoRA with other adapter strategies and continual-learning baselines for language-conditioned imitation. Its reported outcome is policy success under a continual suite curriculum, not source-normalized one-step IDM error against transition retrieval on held-out tasks. | Full paper and experiment section inspected. Before publication, verify exact pretraining/task overlap, per-task data use, and code settings in appendices/released implementation. |
| [FADA (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28476) | Trains a planner–IDM through privileged-oracle distillation and DAgger, then freezes the planner and supervises IDM adaptation using about two minutes of target rollouts. It evaluates IsaacSim-to-MuJoCo dynamics transfer and humanoid hardware. | Directly rules out “adapt only an IDM” as a novel claim. Its question is control under dynamics/embodiment shift with predicted futures; it does not compare few-shot recorded-RGB action prediction on LIBERO against a matched retrieval bank. | Abstract, method algorithm, and stated evaluation domains inspected. Verify full adaptation details, including any low-rank parameterization and rollout count, before precise implementation comparisons. |
| [OpenVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09246) and [OpenVLA-OFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.19645) | Study fine-tuning of a 7B pretrained VLA, including LoRA/compute tradeoffs, and report closed-loop success on LIBERO suites and real-robot tasks. OFT changes decoding, action representation, chunking, and objective. | Supports LoRA as a practical robot-policy adaptation tool, but the backbone scale, pretrained data, action interface, and control-success endpoint differ substantially from a compact visual IDM. It is not a matched baseline. | Primary paper abstracts and official implementation/project results inspected. Do not equate their success percentages or compute with offline MSE/cost here. |
| [HAND Me the Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20455) | Uses one human hand demonstration to retrieve robot-play sub-trajectories through visual filtering and path similarity, then LoRA-fine-tunes a pretrained policy; reports simulation and real-robot success and adaptation time. | Establishes retrieval followed by LoRA policy adaptation and makes broad “retrieval plus adaptation” novelty untenable. It retrieves trajectories across a human/robot data gap rather than matching target IDM transitions or comparing against same-support gradient adaptation. | Full method and experiment sections inspected. Check released code and pretrained-policy settings before directly comparing compute. |
| [REGENT (ICLR 2025)](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/6237474e5a1c58a193af179b2434a86e-Abstract-Conference.html) | Proposes a frozen, in-context retrieval-augmented policy and reports a simple 1-nearest-neighbor retrieval-and-play baseline alongside its learned semi-parametric model. | Supports retrieval as a serious adaptation baseline. Its keys, action-conditioned sequence policy, and evaluation environments differ from cosine retrieval of visual transitions for one-step prediction. | Conference abstract and method summary inspected; full robotics benchmark breakdown remains to be reviewed. |
| [VLA-Pro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29562) | Stores task-specific LoRA adapters as procedural memories and retrieves/fuses them from multimodal context at inference; reports RoboTwin, RLBench, and real-world results. | Rules out novelty for adapter memory/retrieval or dynamically composed LoRA memories. The first study intentionally excludes adapter banks and compares direct transition retrieval with fresh few-shot optimization. | Abstract inspected. | [WIZARD](https://fascetta.github.io/WIZARD/) | Meta-trains from task experts on LIBERO-Goal/Object/10 and tests the held-out fourth suite; infers LoRA weights from language and video without target action labels or test-time gradients. ### What is supported, and what remains open

**Established:** LoRA can adapt robot imitation/VLA policies; retrieval can support in-context transfer; retrieval can be paired with LoRA training; IDM-only target adaptation already exists for humanoid dynamics alignment; and held-out LIBERO generalization with LoRA is already studied. None of those ingredients alone is a novelty claim.

**Still worth testing:** whether, for this frozen visual IDM and a fixed small support set of recorded LIBERO-Goal transitions, updating rank-8 visual LoRA plus the action head improves held-out **action prediction** over (a) the same head updated alone and (b) retrieval using exactly those support transitions—and how much extra compute the difference costs. This is a protocol-specific empirical question, not a claim that LoRA, IDM adaptation, or LIBERO task transfer is new. The two-task initial final-test split is narrow, and checkpoint pretraining exposure must be audited before saying the tasks are unseen beyond this study's source split.

## Older RAID novelty threats and background

RAEA demonstrates retrieved experience incorporated through cross-attention; REGENT blends learned actions with retrieved actions; VINN establishes visual nearest-neighbor action prediction; CLAW retrieves executable actions from planned visual transitions; Seer and UniPi establish future-conditioned inverse dynamics/video plans. They remain relevant when discussing the historical RAID method and any later retrieval extension. See their original references in the historical search below.

Earlier search records for CLAW, RAEA, REGENT, Seer, VINN, DreamGen, UniPi, SIDIL, and related work are preserved below this update as a dated record. Their previous reading-depth statements should not be read as a completed audit of the new adaptation question. Promote TAIL, HAND, FADA, and WIZARD to full-method review before stating the gap.
